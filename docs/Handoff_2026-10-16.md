## 10. Findings and lessons (so they are not repeated)
- **Never mark something verified from our own tool's output.** Verify against the real site. Two real bugs came from this: GetYourGuide showed not-included items (hotel pickup, transport) inside `includes` (the page marks them only with a cross icon), and a single-option activity read as "no options".
- Claude re-sorts tables and invents reasons even when told not to: so the server now returns `rank`, `few_reviews`, `shortlist_for_exact_price`, `coverage`, `price_basis`, and the playbook has a "Comparison checklist". Rating rule: results with fewer than 20 reviews rank after the others and are never Recommended.
- Compare quote prices without profit; a child is a separate price (trip group is 2 adults + child 8: using 3 adults gave a wrong "the quote is cheaper" conclusion).
- Search "from" prices are not final: price the shortlist (cheapest, best rated, most reviewed) of every source with the details tool.
- The automation Chrome clogs when many ad-heavy tabs stay open (Viator, GetYourGuide): close stray tabs through `http://127.0.0.1:9333/json/close/<id>`.
- Playbook edits need a server restart; tunnel URL changes on every full restart.
