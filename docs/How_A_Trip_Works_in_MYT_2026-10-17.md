# How a trip works in MYT, learned by reading the finished trip 435 (quote 273 V4), 17 Oct 2026, read only

Nothing was changed. No traveller or staff names are recorded here. Everything below was seen on the trip's own screens or through our read-only tools.

## The chain, in order
1. **Quotation (versions V1, V2, V3, V4).** Cost lines by category (Transport, Food, Staff, Lodging, Attraction, Other, DMC), each with a profit %, PS or PP pricing, and a currency. The selected version gives the customer price (Gross PP per person and Gross PG for the group; trip 435: EUR 4,730 PP and EUR 9,460 PG for 2 paying travellers). Quote 273 had 29 lines: Transport 12, Staff 3, Lodging 6, Attraction 8.
2. **Supplier reservations = supplier ORDERS.** One order (R-number, "R2338382 - T435 - V2289") per supplier booking; an order can hold several product rows (Arbitrip: 1 order, 6 rows; Get Your Guide: 1 order, 4 rows). Trip 435 has 26 orders in 41 rows. Reservations are NOT the quote lines: they are created from them per supplier, and they keep their own amounts (a quote change does not update them). Extra rows exist that the quote does not have: **rejected rows** (10 "Rejected By Operator", the old attempt that was replaced), **credit rows** (a negative -130.00 row cancels a wrong one) and **staff rows** (commissions paid to people, category Staff).
3. **Order status path.** Ready To Send, then Send Request (emails the supplier) or Skip To COR, then Reservation Confirmed, then **Voucher Issued** (a voucher document is created), then **Voucher In Payment Process**, then **Voucher Paid**. Also **Rejected By Operator**. Trip 435 at 17 Oct: 21 Voucher Paid, 9 Voucher Issued, 10 Rejected, 1 In Payment Process.
4. **Voucher = the accounting document of an order.** Fields: status (Issued, Paid, Payment in Process), doc type Voucher, Doc Ref (a number such as 2289; the screen writes it V2289), issue date, order date, a reference code, supplier, trip, sum in the reservation currency and its shekel equivalent. "View More" shows what the voucher links to: the product, the supplier account (with its balance) and the trip. A voucher covers the WHOLE order: the six Arbitrip rows add up to ILS 12,043.00 = voucher 2295; the four Get Your Guide rows add up to EUR 496.00 = voucher 2296. A cancelled voucher stays visible as a credit with a "-1" suffix and the note "deleted by <user> date <date>" (voucher 1769-1, EUR -132.00).
5. **Supplier payment.** On the order, the PAYMENTS tab lists payments linked to it (order R2150406, 7M Rent a Car, EUR 212.71: "Payment 1945", EUR 212.71, issued 19 Sep 2026, two days before the service on 21 Sep). In Reports > Supplier Payments each payment has a status ("To Match" until it is matched to vouchers), a Matched amount and an Approved box: MYT's own reconciliation is matching payments to vouchers.
6. **Supplier invoice.** Reservations > SUPPLIER INVOICES lists invoices waiting for a payment request; the order row menu has Create/Edit Invoice. (Not read for trip 435.)
7. **Files.** The FILES tab of an order holds Google Drive attachments. Order R2150406 has one PDF, "7m rental car - Confirmation ...". Reading its content needs Google Drive access.
8. **Customer side.** Customer money is Pro Forma Invoices to the customer (trip 435: Doc 243, 1 Feb 2026, ILS 34,298.00, Paid; Doc 271, 18 May 2026, ILS 1,035.00 for an airline reissue add-on, Paid), then receipts. MYT counts income from the "Specified Trip Debit Amount" on each invoice, not the invoice total: ILS 35,333.00 in all.
9. **Trip Balance** (Reports > Balance by trip, #/reports/balanceByTrip/<tripId>, trip-filtered): income vs expenditure, trip vs quotation, customer invoices, expense by supplier, vouchers and one tab per category. Trip 435: income ILS 35,333.00, expenses ILS 27,813.75 (17 vouchers), net ILS 7,519.25 (21.28% margin); quotation group price ILS 33,393.80 and quoted cost ILS 27,718.76. By category (trip against quote, ILS): Transport 8,930 vs 7,662; Lodging 12,043 vs 12,517; Attraction 3,598 vs 4,928; Staff 2,618 vs 2,612; DMC 2,625 vs 0. "Actual Balance" = invoices minus all vouchers; "Accounting Balance" = invoices minus vouchers linked to payment requests. A **Docket Open** button closes the trip's file (not pressed).

## Currency, and why the daily rate matters
- Display currency EUR, accounting currency ILS, ERP foreign currency EUR, extra currency USD. Settings > Accounting Settings > MY CURRENCIES > "Fixing the exchange rate": EUR to ILS, 3.53 on 17 Oct 2026.
- Each voucher keeps the shekel value at the rate in force on its issue date. Read from the vouchers of trip 435 (shekel equivalent divided by the euro sum): 3.681 (Feb and Mar 2026), 3.488 (20 Sep), **3.429 (vouchers issued 9 Oct)**, **3.530 (voucher issued 12 Oct)**. So a change of the rate field changes the shekel value of every voucher issued after it: that is why the client wants it updated daily (this reading is inferred from the sums, not stated by MYT).
- Orders in other currencies exist: Arbitrip, Sixt and some staff rows are in ILS, the rest in EUR. Never add the two together.

## What the actuals show that the quote does not
- **DMC:** RateHawk transfers are quoted under Transport but booked as DMC reservations (ILS 2,625 in DMC against 0 in the quote), which moves ILS 1,268 between categories.
- A finished trip can end very close to its quote in total (expenses ILS 27,814 against quoted ILS 27,719, +0.3%) while single categories move 10 to 30%.

## Consequences for our system (done or to do)
- Per-order, not per-row, matching of vouchers, invoices and payments: DONE (paperwork check, 17 Oct).
- Statuses Voucher Paid, In Payment Process and Rejected By Operator; totals per currency: DONE.
- Closed trips need a different worklist than open ones (everything old is "done"): the report already sets paid and old rejected rows to done.
- Still to build or read: supplier payments and invoices per order (read from the order's PAYMENTS tab, or Trip Balance), the 2 voucher rows cut off in the read, voucher contents from Drive, and the matching status ("To Match") of each payment.

## Round 2 (17 Oct 2026): API endpoints, vouchers, payments, read only

**What the documented public API has (read from MYT's own documentation page, then called for trip 435):** Country, Currency (a list: Id, Name, Symbol, ISOnumber), Customer, Organization, Participant, Tag, Roadmap, Product catalog, History (create), `trip/GetTrips`, `GetTripByTripNumber`, `GetTrip/v3`, `GetTripLog`, `Invoice/GetCustomerInvoicesList`, `reservations/Create` and `Update`, `trip/ChangeTripStatus`. Called on trip 435: `GetCustomerInvoicesList` returns an empty list (the trip's invoices are pro formas) and `GetTripLog` returns nothing. **`GetTrip/v3` itself carries the customer invoices** (`invoices`: number, pro forma flag, total, document date, payable-until, installments with date, amount and currency): our tool `get_customer_invoices` reads them (trip 435: pro forma 243 = ILS 34,298.00 in 9 installments that add up, pro forma 271 = ILS 1,035.00). **Not in the API at all:** vouchers (numbers, status, amounts), supplier payments, supplier invoices, files, balance, the exchange rate. Those come from the screens.

**Vouchers of trip 435 against the orders (all 17 vouchers read from Reports > Balance by trip > Vouchers, trip filtered):** every one of the 17 orders that is not rejected has exactly one voucher and the voucher amount equals the sum of the order's rows (checked with our paperwork tool, tolerance 1.0): Arbitrip 12,043.00 ILS (6 rows), Get Your Guide 496.00 EUR (4 rows), Emerging Travel 269.00 EUR (6 rows), Our Island 721.00 EUR (2 rows) and the single-row orders. The 9 orders without a voucher are the "Rejected By Operator" ones, where none is expected. A voucher links to its order through the order date (the "Order Date" column equals the service date of the order).

**Payments per order (the PAYMENTS tab count, read for all 26 orders):** one payment per product row is the pattern (Arbitrip: 6 rows, 6 payments; Get Your Guide: 4 rows, 4 payments; Emerging Travel: 6 rows, 4 payments while the voucher is only "Issued", so partly paid). Single-row paid orders have 1 payment (7M Rent a Car, SATA x3, Sixt Voucher Paid, Terreiro x2, Our Island, Detours). Sixt "Voucher In Payment Process" has 2 payments. Orders with a voucher but 0 payments in the tab: the two "Voucher Issued" orders (one staff row, FLY MONEY FINTECH) as expected, the credit row, and one staff row marked Voucher Paid (to check on the screen). Five "Rejected By Operator" Get Your Guide orders still show 1 payment each: worth a look (a refund or a credit may be due). The payment amounts and dates were read in full for one order only (7M Rent a Car: Payment 1945, EUR 212.71, 19 Sep 2026).

**How lodging via a platform is booked:** the Arbitrip order holds the six hotel stays of the whole trip as six rows (hotel as product, PS, Room, DBL, BB, ILS), so the supplier on the reservation is the platform, not the hotel. The same is true for RateHawk (Emerging Travel Inc.: transfers and extras as small rows).

## Write API test on trip 168 (17 Oct 2026, approved by the user, test trip only)
- **`reservations/Create` WORKS with our key.** Body as documented: `tripId`, `externalId`, `comments` and `products` (each with `SupplierId`, `StartDate`, `Name`, `PricingTypeId` 1 = PS or 2 = PP, `TypeId` 1-7, `Price`, `CurrencyCodeId`, `Quantity`, `Nights`, `ArrivalTime`, `Comments`). The 14 Oct HTTP 400 was only the missing `products`. Answer: HTTP 200 with the new order id as a list (`[2347217]`). `TypeId` 1 (Transport) worked without category ids. Currency ids from `GET /api/Currency/Get`: ILS 1, USD 2, EUR 3. Read back in `GetTrip/v3` at once: order 2347217, reservation 6716988, price 1.0. A reservation created this way has no voucher, invoice or payment: it starts as a supplier order on the trip.
- **`reservations/Update` WORKS** with `orderDoc` = `{OrderDocId, OrderReservations: [{OrderReservationId, ReservationDate, Quantity, Duration, Price, ArrivalTime, Comments, IsActive}]}`. A body without `ReservationDate` is refused with HTTP 400 "All Reservations must have id and Date". With it: HTTP 200 (returns the order id) and the price read back changed 1.0 to 2.0. Send the quantity and duration unchanged, or they may reset.
- **`trip/ChangeTripStatus` is NOT authorised for our key:** HTTP 401 with an empty body, both with the key in the header and in the URL, sent with `{tripId, statusId}` where `statusId` was the current status (so nothing could change; the status after was still Planning Trip, id 1). Not retried further. Ask MYT support whether the key can be given this right and which field carries the status.
- **No delete endpoint**: the test reservation (order 2347217, "API TEST - delete me (Claude, 17 Oct 2026)", supplier "Lisbon Airport Transfers") must be removed on the MYT screen by a person (Supplier Reservations > row menu > Delete).
- **What this means:** the system can create supplier orders on a trip and correct their price, date and quantity without clicking through the screens (faster and checkable by reading the trip back). It cannot move an order along its status path, issue vouchers or touch payments and invoices (screens only), and cannot change the trip status. Any real use needs the client's written scope and the operator's yes per call; supplier ids come from the trip's quote (never create a supplier).

**Same three tests repeated on trip 906 (status On Road, id 3), approved by the user:** Create HTTP 200 (order 2347218, read back at once, price 1.0), Update HTTP 200 (price read back 2.0), ChangeTripStatus HTTP 401 again with the current status (unchanged). So the rights are the same on both test trips: Create and Update yes, ChangeTripStatus no. Our own tools see an API-created order at once: trip 906 went from 7 to 8 reservations, the new order is "Ready To Send" on the worklist, and the total of the reservations rose from EUR 2,321.00 to EUR 2,323.00. Test orders to delete by hand on the MYT screen: **2347217 (trip 168)** and **2347218 (trip 906)**, both named "API TEST - delete me (Claude, 17 Oct 2026)".
